Sock puppet builder.

A “sock puppet” is a disposable fake identity investigators use to research without exposing themselves. This is a framework + checklist — generate a consistent persona and learn the operational-security rules that keep it from unravelling.

Educational · randomly generated · use only for lawful, authorized research

Never pair a fake name with a real or AI-generated face — both are reverse-image searchable. Use an illustrated avatar or none.

OPSEC checklist

Tick each control as you set it up. One slip links the puppet to you.

The golden rules

1. Separation. A puppet lives in its own browser profile / VM — never your real session.
2. Consistency. Name, age, timezone, writing style and interests must never contradict each other.
3. No crossover. Never log into a real account from the puppet, or vice-versa — shared cookies, IPs and devices deanonymize you.
4. Age it. Brand-new accounts look fake. Let it sit and post mundane things before you engage.
5. Stay legal. A sock puppet is for observation, not entrapment, harassment or fraud. Know the rules of your engagement.
