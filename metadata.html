Why this matters

A single phone photo can leak the exact spot it was taken, the device model, and the precise time. Posting an “anonymous” picture with intact EXIF has deanonymized people, exposed safe houses, and busted fake locations. OSINT investigators check it first.

Protect yourself

Strip metadata before sharing: most phones have a “remove location” share option, or use a scrubbing tool. Note many platforms re-encode uploads and drop EXIF automatically — but never rely on that for sensitive images. Pair with the privacy checklist.
